Welcome to BB's Web Tarot Experiment.


       



A Tarot spread tells a story. Even a simple three-card draw can be read as a small play - Introduction, Crisis, Resolution. The cards depicted here are from the Gendron Tarot, created by Melanie Gendron. In my opinion it is one of the most beautifully realized decks ever produced. The artist spent five years meditating on and crafting these images, and I think that devotion is reflected in every card.

This application is a personal programming exercise and a small tribute to an exceptional work of art. It is not for profit. If you enjoy what you see, please consider supporting the artist by seeking out a copy - the Gendron Tarot is currently out of print, but new and like-new copies can still be found on Amazon.
